Not a lock. A nudge.

EnoughWork isn't designed to control you. There are no punishments, forced lockouts, productivity scores, streaks, or guilt systems. The goal is simple: help you notice when it's time to stop.

The overlay is intentionally easy to dismiss because awareness matters more than restriction. Sometimes a small interruption is all you need to finally stand up, stretch, rest, or call it a day.

I built this because I kept working for hours without noticing. One task became another, and suddenly half the day was gone.
